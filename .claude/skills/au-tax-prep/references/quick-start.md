# Quick-start guide

Show the section below verbatim to users who haven't yet provided documents or who ask how to use this skill. Keep any additions brief — the point is to get them set up in under a minute of reading.

---

## How to prepare your tax pack — 3 steps

**1. Gather your documents into one folder, ideally grouped like this:**

- `Income/` — payment summaries / income statements, bank interest statements
- `Property/<address>/` — agent statements, loan interest statements, council rates, insurance, depreciation schedule (one subfolder per property)
- `Shares-ETF/` — dividend statements, annual tax statements (AMMA/AMIT), buy & sell contract notes
- `Crypto/` — exchange transaction reports, or a report from a crypto tax tool (Koinly, CryptoTaxCalculator, …)
- `Work-expenses/` — receipts, work-from-home hours diary, car logbook
- `Sole-trader/` — invoices you issued, business expense receipts, asset purchase invoices
- `Prior-year/` — last year's tax return or accountant pack (supplies carry-forward items like capital losses)
- `SMSF/` — keep fund documents completely separate from personal ones

PDFs, scans/photos, CSV and Excel files all work. Don't rename or edit the files — originals stay traceable.

**2. Give Claude access** — connect the folder (or attach the files) in this chat.

**3. Type what you want**, for example:

> *"Create my tax accountant pack for FY2025-26"*

or just one category:

> *"Summarise my investment property expenses for FY26"*

Claude will then confirm the scope, ask a few clarifying questions (like work-use percentages for mixed personal/work expenses), and build an accountant-ready Excel workbook plus a one-page cover brief for your registered tax agent.

*This organises your records — it is not tax advice, and a registered tax agent must validate everything before lodging.*
