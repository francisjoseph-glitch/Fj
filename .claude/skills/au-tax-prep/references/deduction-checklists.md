# Persona-based deduction discovery checklists

Purpose: surface documents and records the user forgot they had. Ask the groups matching the user's occupation/investor profile (from Workflow step 1) as **one compact set of questions**, not a drip. These checklists never assert deductibility — that judgement stays with the registered tax agent. Record "user confirmed none" for empty groups so the accountant knows the question was asked. Where an item has mixed personal/work use, it feeds the apportionment gate (Workflow step 5).

## All employees (ask everyone with PAYG income)

- Union or professional association membership fees
- Income protection insurance premiums (outside super)
- Donations to deductible gift recipients (receipts)
- Cost of managing tax affairs (last year's accountant fee, tax software)
- Personal super contributions they intend to claim (notice-of-intent acknowledgment from the fund — flag if missing)
- Work-related subscriptions, journals, licences, certifications, working-with-children / police checks
- Home internet and mobile phone used for work (→ apportionment)
- Tools, equipment, home-office furniture, laptops/devices (items >$300 need depreciation treatment — flag)

## Engineers and technical professionals

- Professional registration and chartered status fees (e.g. Engineers Australia, BPEQ, APESMA union)
- Technical software licences and cloud subscriptions used for work
- Standards purchases (e.g. Australian Standards documents)
- Protective clothing / safety gear (hard hats, boots, hi-vis) and laundry of it
- Site travel between workplaces (not home-to-work commuting — flag ambiguous trips)
- Conferences, short courses, self-education linked to current role (→ apportionment if mixed purpose)
- Calculators, drafting tools, field equipment

## Managers and business leaders

- Professional memberships (e.g. AICD, AIM, CPA/CA if applicable)
- Executive coaching or self-education tied to current income-earning role (flag if arguably for a *new* role — accountant call)
- Networking event and seminar costs (flag entertainment components — generally not deductible)
- Home office running costs for after-hours work (→ apportionment)
- Briefcase/laptop bag, devices, board-papers subscriptions
- Reportable fringe benefits and reportable super on the income statement (transcribe — relevant to the accountant for Div 293 and offset thresholds; do not compute anything)

## Residential and commercial property investors

- Loan interest statements for each loan (flag mixed-purpose or redrawn loans → apportionment)
- Depreciation / quantity surveyor schedule (suggest they obtain one if absent — flag, don't insist)
- Land tax assessments, council rates, water, insurance, body corporate levies
- Property manager annual summary (reconcile against monthly statements)
- Repairs invoices (repair vs capital improvement → always flag for accountant)
- Borrowing costs on new loans (spread over 5 years → Carry-forward tab)
- Travel to inspect property (generally not deductible for residential since 2017 — record and flag; commercial differs — accountant call)
- Periods of private use or below-market rent (→ apportionment / flag)

## Share / ETF investors

- Dividend statements including DRP notices (shares issued in lieu are still income)
- Annual tax statements for every ETF/trust holding (AMMA/AMIT)
- Buy AND sell contract notes for any disposal (missing buy notes = cost base gap — flag)
- Interest on margin or investment loans (→ apportionment if mixed)
- Financial advice fees related to existing investments (initial advice differs — flag for accountant), portfolio admin/platform fees, investment journal subscriptions
- Franking credits and TFN withholding shown on statements
- Prior-year capital losses carried forward (→ Carry-forward tab)

## Crypto investors

- Full transaction history export per exchange/wallet, or a crypto tax tool report
- Records of crypto-to-crypto swaps, spending, and gifting (all CGT events)
- Staking, airdrop, interest and mining receipts (ordinary income at receipt — list separately)
- Lost/stolen asset records (exchange collapse, wallet loss — flag for accountant)
- Fees paid in crypto (record AUD value)
- Prior-year capital losses carried forward (→ Carry-forward tab)

## Commodity investors (gold, silver, etc.)

- Dealer invoices for every purchase and sale (both sides needed for CGT)
- Storage and insurance costs for investment holdings
- Personal-use-asset question for acquisitions under $10,000 — record and flag, never decide

## Sole traders

Run the full sole-trader category in `references/categories.md`; additionally ask about:

- Invoices issued but unpaid at 30 June (cash vs accruals — flag for accountant)
- Home used as place of business (→ apportionment; occupancy costs raise CGT questions — flag)
- Vehicle used for business (logbook or km record → apportionment)
- Assets bought during the year (instant asset write-off threshold verified in Workflow step 2 — flag eligibility, don't decide)
- Super contributions for themselves (notice of intent)
- Bad debts written off, insurance (public liability, professional indemnity, income protection)
- PSI — if most income comes from one client for personal effort/skills, flag prominently for the accountant; never classify it yourself
