# plan.md

30-day plan for @financewithfrancis_, 26 Sep to 25 Oct 2026. Built from the audit
of 37 posts (6 May to 22 Sep) pulled on 25 Sep. Read by the other Instagram skills.
Nothing here is scheduled or posted. Francis runs it.

## What the audit found (the evidence this plan is built on)

- Median views 622. Offset reel (29 Jul) did 28,315, a 45.5x outlier.
- 70% of views in the last 30 days came from non-followers, but the account sits at
  201 followers. Content travels; the profile does not convert. Fix the profile first.
- Reels since 20 Aug: median views 485 (was 1,080), hold at 3s 39% (was 48%),
  average watch 5.6s (was 7.5s). The hooks got softer.
- Winners: own mistakes and costs, specific numbers, one counterintuitive lending fact.
- Losers: generic topical openers ("with rates...", "spring season...", "got
  pre-approval?") and soft reassurance posts.
- Only format that gets sent: personality ("lawyer energy" reel, 16 sends per 1,000 reach).
- Best follow converter: the "Hey, I'm Francis" intro post (174 profile visits, 13 follows).

## Rules for every post this month

1. First line on screen and first spoken line do the same job, inside 2 seconds.
   Test: could a big generic finance account have posted this opener? If yes, rewrite.
2. Your own story or a number, never a client story. Verbatim questions from DMs or
   stories are fine if nothing identifies the person.
3. One idea per reel. No numbered lists of five.
4. Choose the cover. Four words of text, readable at thumbnail size.
5. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
6. General information note on every post. Wording to be confirmed with CBA compliance.
7. Check any figure (scheme caps, stamp duty thresholds, HECS rules, rates) against
   the official source on the day of posting.
