## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Straight-talking property and personal finance education from a Melbourne home lending specialist who also invests.

**What it does:** Finance with Francis (@FinancewithFrancis on Instagram) explains how home loans, borrowing power, property investing and wealth building actually work in Australia. Content comes from someone who deals with first home buyers, refinancers and investors every day and runs his own investment portfolio around Melbourne.

**Product category:** Australian personal finance and property education content (finfluencer / creator education).

**Product type:** Content brand and personal brand. Free social content first. [CONFIRM] any paid or lead products (newsletter, guides, courses, coaching, referrals).

**Business model:** [CONFIRM] Current monetisation, if any. Audience growth and trust are the working goal until confirmed.

## Target Audience

**Who:** Australians, weighted to Melbourne and Victoria, who are trying to buy, refinance or invest in property.

**Primary use case:** Understand the lending and property decisions in front of them well enough to act with confidence.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Work out how much I can borrow and what's stopping me borrowing more
- Get into my first home sooner (deposit, schemes, grants, LMI trade-offs)
- Know whether my current loan is still competitive and whether to refinance
- Build a property portfolio without overextending

**Use cases:**
- First home buyer planning a deposit and timeline
- Owner-occupier checking their rate and loan structure
- Investor planning the next purchase, equity release or structure
- Young professional starting to think about wealth building

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Lending and property decisions are high stakes, jargon heavy and full of conflicting online advice.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic finfluencers lack inside knowledge of how lenders assess applications
- Bank and broker marketing is sales led
- Forums and comment sections are loud and often wrong

**What it costs them:** Missed borrowing capacity, overpaying on interest, delayed purchases, poorly structured loans.

**Emotional tension:** Fear of getting it wrong on the biggest purchase of their life, frustration at feeling priced out, anxiety about rates.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Other Australian property and personal finance creators. Often general or hype driven; fewer have daily lending experience. [CONFIRM] specific accounts Francis benchmarks against.

**Secondary:** Mortgage broker and bank content, comparison sites. Useful but sales or product led.

**Indirect:** Asking family and friends, forums, doing nothing.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Works in home lending day to day, so knows how applications are actually assessed
- Invests personally, so speaks from experience not theory
- Commerce background, direct no-fluff communication style

**Why customers choose us:** Credible, practical, and no sales pressure.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "You work for a bank, so you're biased" | Education only, no product pushing. Be transparent about role. |
| "Property is out of reach anyway" | Show realistic paths and the numbers behind them. |
| "Isn't this just general advice?" | Yes, and say so clearly. It's education to help people ask better questions. |

**Anti-persona:** People wanting get-rich-quick property schemes, or personal advice on their specific situation through DMs.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Tired of hype and generic content that doesn't reflect how lending works.
**Pull:** Insider but independent-feeling explanations from a practitioner.
**Habit:** Following bigger, established finance accounts.
**Anxiety:** Whether the information is trustworthy and current.

## Customer Language

**How they describe the problem:** [CONFIRM] Pull verbatim phrases from comments and DMs. Likely: "how much can I borrow", "is now a good time to buy", "should I refinance".

**Words to use:** borrowing power, serviceability, deposit, LMI, equity, offset, fixed vs variable, first home buyer.

**Words to avoid:** hype ("financial freedom fast", "hack"), guarantees, anything that reads as personal advice or a product recommendation.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Professional but warm, like a straight-talking colleague. Not corporate, not overly matey.
**Style:** Direct. Lead with the answer. Plain language, real numbers, Australian context.
**Mechanics:** Australian spelling, metric units, no em dashes, no slang unless the audience uses it first.
**Personality:** Credible, practical, candid, grounded, approachable.

## Compliance Guardrails

Treat this section as non-negotiable for all content and campaigns.

- Content is general information and education only. Include a general-information disclaimer and never frame content as personal financial or credit advice.
- Never recommend specific credit products or lenders, or make rate or approval promises.
- Francis is employed by CBA. Follow CBA's social media, outside-activities and conflict-of-interest policies, and be transparent about the role where relevant. [CONFIRM] exact disclosure wording with CBA compliance.
- Never use customer information, stories or data from lending work, even anonymised, without clearance.
- Check current figures (rates, scheme caps, stamp duty thresholds, grant amounts) against official sources at time of posting; they change often.
